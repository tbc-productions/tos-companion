TOS Companion

Welcome! Add your TerraMaster NAS address below to get started. Use the address you open TOS 7 at in Chrome, e.g. https://192.168.1.10:5443. Chrome will ask you to allow access to it.

TOS Companion only talks to the addresses you add here, using your existing TOS sign-in. Nothing is sent anywhere else. It is not affiliated with TerraMaster.

Your NAS

If you reach the same NAS at more than one address, like a domain and a local IP, add them all to that NAS with “Add address”. The popup uses whichever one works, preferring one you’re signed in to. The default NAS is used by the popup and the badge.
