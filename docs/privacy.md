# TOS Companion for TerraMaster: privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

TOS Companion is a Chrome and Microsoft Edge extension for the web interface of your own TerraMaster NAS (TOS 7).

**What it accesses.** It accesses only the NAS addresses you add in its settings. To make signed-in requests to your NAS, it uses the TOS sign-in cookie already stored in your browser. Through those requests it reads information from your NAS, such as installed apps, notifications, Docker containers, system stats and volumes, and it carries out the actions you ask for.

**What it stores.** Your settings and NAS addresses are saved in your browser's extension storage, which syncs to your Google or Microsoft account if browser sync is on. A cache of app icons, Docker status and Overview data is saved on your computer only. You can clear the cache at any time in settings. Your TOS password is never stored.

**What it shares.** Nothing. The extension sends data only to your own NAS. It has no servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never sells or shares data with anyone.

**Removing data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue on the [project's GitHub repository](https://github.com/tbc-productions/tos-companion/issues).

**Source code.** The extension is open source: [github.com/tbc-productions/tos-companion](https://github.com/tbc-productions/tos-companion).

TOS Companion is not affiliated with TerraMaster.
